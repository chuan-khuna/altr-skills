unslop

Remove the phrases and habits that make text sound machine-written, and keep what it means.

skills/writing/unslop manual only

What it does

unslop edits text to remove AI tells. It scans for the patterns in a numbered rule list, then rewrites. It keeps the meaning and matches the tone the text is meant to have.

The rule numbers are stable ids. Other skills cite them: resume says “unslop 16” for bold labels and “unslop 19” for straight quotes. A removed rule leaves a gap in the numbers, so the citations stay correct.

The skill comes from cursor/plugins pstack, pinned at commit e1cc031.

The skill sets disable-model-invocation: true. Claude does not start it by itself. You call it by name, or another skill tells Claude to read it.

How it works

  1. Scan. The skill checks the text against each numbered rule.
  2. Rewrite. It rewrites each line that fails a rule. It keeps the meaning and the intended tone.

The rules, by kind

  • Words. No AI vocabulary such as delve, crucial, pivotal or showcase (7). “Is” or “has”, not “serves as” or “boasts” (8). The plain word, not “leverage” (31). No abstract metaphor nouns such as paradigm or flywheel (26).
  • Sentence shapes. No trailing “-ing” phrase that adds no fact (3). No “not just X, but Y” (9). Ideas do not come in forced threes (10). One idea per sentence (28). Active voice (29).
  • Claims. No vague attributions such as “experts believe” (5). One hedge at most (24). No generic conclusions (25). Say what it does, not how it feels (27).
  • Punctuation and format. No em dashes or en dashes (13). A colon goes only before a list or an example (14). Sentence case headings (17). Straight quotes (19). No decorative emojis (18).
  • Chat habits. No “I hope this helps!” (20). No praise or agreement openers (22). Cut filler such as “in order to” (23).

What it writes

The skill returns the edited text. It writes no file of its own. An example:

Before: Great question! This pivotal update serves as a testament to our commitment,
        ensuring a seamless experience — not just faster, but smarter.
After:  The update cuts page load from 2.1 s to 0.8 s.

In the example, the 0.8 s figure must come from the writer. Rule 27 asks for the number. It does not invent one.

Common questions

Why are some rule numbers missing?
Other skills cite rules by number. When a rule is removed, its number is not reused, so old citations do not point at the wrong rule.
Does it change the meaning?
No. It removes patterns and keeps the meaning and tone.
Can another skill override a rule?
Yes. resume replaces rule 33 for abbreviations: it keeps well-known ones such as SQL and API as they are.

It’s working if

  • No em dashes, curly quotes or decorative emojis remain.
  • None of the listed AI words, such as delve or crucial, remain.
  • Each vague claim is now a number, a name or a mechanism, or is gone.
  • The text says the same thing as before, in the same tone.

Where it fits

unslop is the base layer of the writing group. technical-writing hands it the slop-pattern catalogue, and resume checks every line against its rules by number.
